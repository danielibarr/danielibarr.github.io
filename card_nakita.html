NAKITA "Mr.Spin 3000" | Technical Illustration

This project was developed as part of a technical illustration assignment, with the objective of creating a detailed visual representation of a product and presenting it in an appealing advertising format. The proposal involved designing a fictional power drill for the imaginary brand Nakita, model "Mr. Spin 3000", combining technical precision with a retro-modern graphic approach.


The process began with the digital illustration of the drill, carefully working on proportions, volumes, and details to convey realism and solidity. Using Adobe Illustrator, I developed each component with clean lines, subtle shadows, and highlights, achieving a balance between technical accuracy and visual appeal.


For the final composition, I designed an advertising poster that combines custom typography, dynamic graphics, and a color scheme that highlights the product. The result is a piece that not only communicates the drill’s technical specifications—motor, speed, drilling capacity, and weight—but also conveys a strong and trustworthy brand identity.


This project was a comprehensive exercise that merged the precision of technical illustration with the creativity of advertising design, demonstrating how a product can come to life through digital art.
