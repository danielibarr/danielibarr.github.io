DRACULA | Cover Book

In this fascinating project, I had the opportunity to visually reinterpret Bram Stoker's iconic novel, 'Dracula'. Using Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator, I embarked on the task of creating a complete book cover design, encompassing the front cover, spine, and back cover. The goal was to create a comprehensive cover design that would evoke the dark and imposing essence of the main character, crafted to connect with readers of this classic work of gothic horror.


The cover focused on evoking the imposing presence of Count Dracula, drawing particular inspiration from the distinctive, intense red armor seen in Francis Ford Coppola's classic film adaptation (1992). This intimidating and detailed armor became the central element of the composition, conveying the character's strength and danger. The color palette, dominated by intense red and orange tones, complements the dramatic and mysterious atmosphere. This is accompanied by a large title of the novel in a unique typography that emulates the art of the time and place where the novel's story unfolds.


The book's spine was designed to be legible and cohesive, displaying the author's name, 'Bram Stoker's,' and the novel's title in an elegant, matching typeface. On the back cover, a plot summary was included to offer readers an introduction to the story, along with a small illustration that adds an extra visual element to the narrative.


This project combined graphic design and image manipulation to create a cover that is not only visually appealing but also captures the essence of the literary work. The attention to detail, from the choice of typography to the illustration of the armor, contributes to a final result that is both coherent and representative of the legendary story of Dracula.
