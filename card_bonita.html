La Bonita! | Restaurant Menu (Branding)

This project was developed as part of an InDesign assignment focused on the design of the menu, visual identity, and graphic applications for a fictional restaurant. La Bonita is a high-end Mexican restaurant whose image is inspired by the artisanal richness of Puebla’s talavera pottery.


The proposal was built around a minimalist yet powerful palette: a deep navy blue with character, paired with a warm white that evokes elegance without losing vibrancy. Decorative patterns, inspired by traditional talavera tiles, were reinterpreted with a contemporary approach, creating compositions and repetitions that bring visual rhythm and a unique personality to the brand.


The logo features the elegant silhouette of a dove in profile, facing right, in a pose that suggests the moment just before taking flight. Its open wings convey freedom, dynamism, and projection, while its slightly left-shifted position creates a harmonious visual balance over the restaurant’s name.


In addition to the menu design, the identity was applied to various elements such as take-out bags, business cards, and complementary graphic pieces, ensuring consistency and strong visual impact at every customer touchpoint.


This project was a comprehensive branding exercise in which Mexican tradition and craftsmanship merged with a modern graphic language to bring to life an elegant, memorable, and authentic identity.
