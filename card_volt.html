Volt: Blue Lightning | Popcan 3D Render

As part of an assignment for my Adobe Illustrator class, I was challenged to create the visual identity for a fictional energy drink and present it through a realistic 3D mockup on an aluminum can.


The proposal, titled "Volt | Blue Lightning", is an imaginary energy drink brand designed to convey energy, strength, and dynamism through a vibrant and striking graphic style. To achieve this, I used a contrasting color palette dominated by vibrant blue and intense yellow, combined with a geometric pattern inspired by electric bolts that reinforces the brand’s identity and its concept of instant power.


The label design combines an electric pattern of angular lines with custom typography, emphasizing the youthful and competitive character of the product. Once the vector illustration was complete, the next step was to bring it into a three-dimensional environment. I integrated the label into a 3D can model using mapping, lighting, and shading techniques to simulate a realistic result—ideal for advertising presentations or commercial proposals.


This project was not only a technical challenge in vector illustration and modeling but also a creative process in which I aimed to balance aesthetics, functionality, and visual coherence in the product’s branding.
